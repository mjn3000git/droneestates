Pricing and Packages

Every property is different, so each package has a range. Your final quote is confirmed after you send an inquiry, before anything is booked.

  • BasicAerial photos plus a short clip.$250 to $350
  • StandardEverything in Basic, plus a full aerial listing video and a lot overview.$475 to $695
  • PremiumEverything in Standard, plus a roof and lot report (a visual review, not a home inspection) and a short written summary.$750 to $995

Travel is measured in road miles from Queensbury: no charge up to 15 miles and in Schroon Lake*, $40 from 15 to 30, $75 from 30 to 45, and $125 from 45 to 60 on scheduled days. We do not travel past 60 road miles.

*Schroon Lake is included in Zone 1 at no travel charge because we're there often and schedule visits around regular trips. Same-day requests have the same-day fee no matter where you are.
