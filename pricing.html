Pricing and Packages

Every property is different, so each package has a range. Where your job lands depends on the size of the home, the size of the lot, and how much there is to cover. These ranges help you plan. Your final quote is confirmed after you send an inquiry, before anything is booked.

Tier 01

Basic

$250 to $350

Aerial photos plus a short clip.

  • 15 to 25 edited aerial photos
  • A 15 to 30 second aerial clip, sized for MLS and social media
  • Delivery in 2 business days
Tier 02

Standard

$475 to $695

Everything in Basic, plus:

  • A full aerial listing video, 60 to 90 seconds, with music
  • A lot overview image showing the approximate property lines and key features
Tier 03

Premium

$750 to $995

Everything in Standard, plus:

  • A roof and lot report: a visual review with marked-up aerial images showing visible wear, damage, drainage and other items worth a closer look
  • A short written summary you can share with buyers, sellers or a contractor

What moves a price within the range

  • Home size and roof complexity
  • Lot size (packages cover up to 5 acres)
  • Extra buildings such as a garage, barn or shed
  • Tree cover, terrain or airspace that takes more flight time
